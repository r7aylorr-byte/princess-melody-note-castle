# Princess Melody's Note Castle

A touch-friendly music-reading castle for ages 6–8. Princesses, rainbows, butterflies, and a charm-chest reward shop. Works in Safari on iPhone and iPad. Progress stays on the device.

Play it: https://r7aylorr-byte.github.io/princess-melody-note-castle/

## Add to iPhone or iPad home screen

1. Open the site in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button.
3. Tap Add to Home Screen.
4. Name it Note Castle.

## What she practices

- The five lines and four spaces of the treble staff
- Line notes: Every Good Butterfly Does Fly (E G B D F)
- Space notes: Fairy Always Collects Emeralds (F A C E)
- Middle C on her little balcony line
- Twinkle, Twinkle and Mary Had a Little Lamb

Stars open a Charm Chest. Treasures are original castle charms: bag-charm buddies, tiny halo pals, claw clips, bead bracelets, and surprise-box secrets.
